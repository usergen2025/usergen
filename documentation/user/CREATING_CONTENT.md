# Creating Content

## Overview
Learn how to create amazing AI-powered content using UserGen.ai.

## Types of Content

### Video Generation
Create professional videos using AI:
1. Click "Create Video" from your dashboard
2. Enter your script or prompts
3. Select style and settings
4. Click "Generate"
5. Wait for processing (usually 1-3 minutes)
6. Preview and download your video

### Avatar Creation
Generate custom avatars:
1. Click "Create Avatar"
2. Describe your desired avatar
3. Choose style and characteristics
4. Click "Generate"
5. Download your avatar

### Other Content Types
- Scripts
- Images
- Audio content

## Tips for Best Results

### For Videos:
- Write clear, descriptive scripts
- Specify the tone (professional, casual, energetic)
- Choose appropriate styles
- Keep scripts concise for best results

### For Avatars:
- Be specific in descriptions
- Mention style preferences (realistic, cartoon, abstract)
- Describe personality traits
- Provide style references if possible

## Managing Your Content

### Viewing Your Content
- All your generated content appears in "My Content"
- Filter by type (video, avatar, etc.)
- Search by name or date

### Downloading
- Click the download button on any content
- Choose format and quality
- Save to your device

### Sharing
- Share directly to social media
- Copy shareable links
- Embed in websites

## Content Limits

- Content generation costs credits (see [Understanding Credits](./UNDERSTANDING_CREDITS.md))
- Check your credit balance before generating
- Some content types may have size or duration limits

## Troubleshooting

### Content Not Generating
- Check your credit balance
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection
- Try again if the service is busy

### Quality Issues
- Refine your prompts for better results
- Try different styles
- Adjust generation settings

## Best Practices

1. **Save Credits** - Preview before generating final versions
2. **Be Specific** - Clear descriptions yield better results
3. **Experiment** - Try different styles and approaches
4. **Organize** - Name your content clearly
5. **Backup** - Download important content
