# Getting Started Guide

## Welcome!

Welcome to UserGen.ai! This guide will help you create your account and start generating amazing AI-powered content.

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Sign Up
1. Visit the UserGen.ai website
2. Click on "Sign Up" or "Get Started"
3. Enter your information:
   - Name
   - Email address
   - Password (must be at least 8 characters)
   - Optional: Phone number
4. Click "Create Account"

### Step 2: Verify Your Email
1. Check your email inbox
2. Click the verification link in the email
3. You'll be redirected back to UserGen.ai

### Step 3: Complete Your Profile
1. Add a profile picture (optional)
2. Tell us about your goals (optional)
3. You're ready to start!

## Your Free Credits

When you sign up, you receive **100 free credits** to get started. Use these credits to explore and create your first content.

## First Steps

### 1. Explore the Dashboard
Take a moment to familiarize yourself with:
- Your credit balance (shown in the top right)
- Navigation menu
- Create content buttons

### Step 2: Generate Your First Content
1. Click "Create Video" or "Create Avatar"
2. Follow the prompts
3. Watch your content being generated
4. Download or share when ready

## Understanding Your Account

### Individual Account
- Your personal account
- Has its own credits
- Perfect for personal projects

### Workspaces
- Create teams for collaboration
- Share credits with team members
- Perfect for organizations
