# Understanding Credits

## What are Credits?

Credits are the currency used in UserGen.ai to generate content. Each action (video generation, avatar creation, etc.) costs a certain number of credits.

## Your Credit Balance

### Individual Credits
- Shown in the top right of your dashboard
- Used when working in "My Account" mode
- Personal to your account

### Workspace Credits
- Shown in workspace dashboard
- Shared among workspace members
- Used when working in workspace mode

## Credit Costs

### Common Actions:
- **Video Generation**: 10 credits
- **Avatar Creation**: 5 credits
- Prices may vary for other content types

**Note:** Check current pricing in the app as costs may change.

## Getting More Credits

### Purchasing Credits
1. Click "Buy Credits" button
2. Choose a credit package
3. Complete payment
4. Credits are added instantly to your account

### Packages
Various credit packages are available:
- Small packages for occasional use
- Medium packages for regular use
- Large packages for businesses

## Using Credits

### Automatic Deduction
Credits are automatically deducted when:
- You generate content
- You use premium features
- You perform credit-costing actions

### Preventive Checks
- The system checks your balance before generating
- If insufficient credits, you'll see a warning
- You'll need to purchase credits to continue

## Tracking Credit Usage

### View Your Transactions
1. Go to "Credits" or "Transactions"
2. View all credit transactions:
   - Credits spent on content
   - Credits purchased
   - Credits earned (if applicable)
   - Credits refunded (if applicable)

### Credit History
See detailed history of:
- Date and time of transactions
- Type of transaction (spent/earned/purchased)
- Amount of credits
- Description (what it was used for)

## Free Credits

### Welcome Bonus
- New accounts receive 100 free credits
- Use these to explore and create your first content
- No credit card required

### Promotional Credits
- Keep an eye out for promotional offers
- Special events may include bonus credits
- Check your email for exclusive offers

## Credit Tips

1. **Monitor Balance** - Keep track of your credit usage
2. **Purchase in Advance** - Buy credits when you have projects planned
3. **Use Wisely** - Preview content before final generation to save credits
4. **Team Credits** - Consider workspace credits for team projects
5. **Check Promotions** - Look for special offers

## Refunds

### When Credits May Be Refunded:
- Service errors or failures
- Content generation failures
- Cancelled orders (under certain conditions)

Contact support if you believe you're eligible for a refund.
