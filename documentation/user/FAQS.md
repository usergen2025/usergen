# Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Registration

### How do I create an account?
Visit the UserGen.ai website and click "Sign Up". Enter your name, email, and password to get started. You'll receive 100 free credits upon registration.

### Do I need to verify my email?
Yes, email verification is required. Check your inbox for the verification link after signing up.

### Can I change my email address?
Yes, you can update your email address in your account settings.

### What if I forget my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

## Credits

### How do credits work?
Credits are the currency used to generate content. Each content generation costs a certain number of credits. Check [Understanding Credits](./UNDERSTANDING_CREDITS.md) for details.

### How do I buy more credits?
Click "Buy Credits" in your dashboard and choose a package. Credits are added instantly after payment.

### What if I run out of credits?
You'll need to purchase more credits to continue generating content. The system will notify you when your balance is low.

### Can I get a refund for unused credits?
Please review our refund policy in the terms of service or contact support for specific cases.

## Content Generation

### How long does content generation take?
Most content is generated within 1-3 minutes, though complex content may take longer.

### Can I cancel a generation in progress?
Yes, you can cancel ongoing generations, but credits may still be deducted depending on progress.

### What formats can I download?
Available formats depend on content type. Common formats include MP4 for videos and PNG/JPG for images.

### Can I edit generated content?
You can regenerate content with different prompts or download and edit externally.

## Workspaces

### What is a workspace?
A workspace is a collaborative space where team members can work together, share credits, and manage projects.

### How do I create a workspace?
Go to "Workspaces" and click "Create Workspace". Enter a name and description, then invite members.

### How do I invite team members?
In your workspace, go to "Members" and click "Invite Member". Enter their email and choose their role.

### What are the different member roles?
- **Owner**: Full control
- **Admin**: Can manage members and settings
- **Member**: Can create content
- **Viewer**: View-only access

### Can I leave a workspace?
Yes, you can leave a workspace from the workspace settings. You cannot leave if you're the owner (transfer ownership first).

## Technical Issues

### Why is my content not generating?
Common causes:
- Insufficient credits
- Poor internet connection
- Service temporarily unavailable

Try checking your credit balance and internet connection first.

### What if I get an error message?
Note the error message and try again. If it persists, contact support with the error details.

### Is my data secure?
Yes, we take data security seriously. See our privacy policy for details on how we protect your information.

### Can I use UserGen.ai on mobile?
Yes, UserGen.ai works on mobile browsers. We recommend using the latest version of your mobile browser.

## Billing & Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit cards and other payment methods as listed during checkout.

### Will I be charged automatically?
No, credits are purchased on-demand. There are no automatic recurring charges unless you subscribe to a plan.

### Can I see my billing history?
Yes, you can view your transaction history in the "Credits" or "Billing" section of your account.

## Support

### How do I contact support?
Use the "Contact Support" option in your account or email support@usergen.ai.

### What information should I include when contacting support?
- Your account email
- Description of the issue
- Screenshots (if applicable)
- Error messages (if any)

### How quickly will I get a response?
We aim to respond within 24 hours on business days.
